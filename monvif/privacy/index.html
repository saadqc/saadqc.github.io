Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

A clear account of what the app accesses, where information stays, and what you control.

Privacy contact

Your cameras connect to your device. Monvif does not send your footage or camera passwords to us.

Monvif is published by Zehna Tech. It lets you view and control cameras and recorders you configure. The Android app uses the package name com.zehna.monvif. This policy covers the Monvif app and this website, including internal-testing releases.

Information the app uses

To connect to your cameras, Monvif handles the addresses and ports you enter, camera names and groups, usernames and passwords, device information, stream profiles, and connection settings. It also uses your viewing preferences, such as layouts, selected channels, theme, and orientation.

Connection details and preferences are stored locally on your device. Camera credentials are stored through the operating system’s secure-storage facilities. Monvif does not require a Zehna Tech account.

Camera connections and controls

Monvif communicates directly with the cameras, recorders, and stream addresses you configure. Local discovery sends network discovery requests to find compatible ONVIF devices. Playback requests, camera settings, and pan, tilt, and zoom (PTZ) commands are sent to the selected device.

Connections can leave your local network if you configure a remote address. The camera or recorder receives the information needed to authenticate and respond, and may keep its own access logs. Its owner or service provider controls those practices.

Some camera protocols, including ordinary HTTP and RTSP, are not encrypted. Monvif does not add end-to-end encryption to those connections. Use cameras you are authorized to access and protect remote access with a trusted network or VPN.

Video, audio, and snapshots

Live video and audio, and recorded footage requested from a recorder, are received and processed on your device for playback. Zehna Tech does not operate a server that receives, relays, or stores these feeds.

When you take a snapshot, Monvif saves an image on your device. Its filename can include the camera name and capture time. On supported Android devices, the app also saves a copy to the gallery under Pictures/Monvif.

If you open a snapshot in another app, Monvif grants that app access to the selected image. Copies you export, share, or back up are handled by the destination app or service under its own privacy practices.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

Monvif does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or automatic crash-report uploads to Zehna Tech. We do not collect camera feeds, camera passwords, snapshots, or app activity on a Zehna Tech server, and we do not sell this information.

The app can produce local diagnostic messages about startup, connection timing, and errors. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information you send and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send camera passwords or private footage.

Permissions

Network, Wi-Fi state, and multicast access support camera connections and discovery. Local-network access may require approval on your operating system. Gallery and file access support saving and opening snapshots.

Monvif’s camera-monitoring features do not require access to your phone’s own camera or microphone, contacts, or precise location. Camera audio comes from the stream you select.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Saved camera entries and preferences remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app’s data. You can remove cameras in the app and delete snapshots from Library. Deleting a snapshot in Monvif also attempts to remove its Android gallery copy.

Deleting a gallery image alone may leave Monvif’s private copy in Library. Copies exported to other apps, shared with other people, or stored in backups must be managed separately. Deleting an image in Monvif does not remove footage stored on your camera or recorder.

Your operating system may back up, synchronize, retain, or restore app data and secure-storage items, depending on your device and account settings. Uninstalling generally removes app-private files, but gallery copies, backups, and some operating-system credential records may remain. Use your device’s storage, gallery, backup, and credential controls to manage those copies.

Children’s privacy

Monvif is not directed to children under 13. Zehna Tech does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us.

This site does not add analytics, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site.
