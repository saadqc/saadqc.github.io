Privacy Policy

Monvif is designed to monitor cameras without routing your video, credentials, or snapshots through a Mimigo-operated server.

Who we are

Mimigo publishes Monvif, an application for viewing and managing user-configured ONVIF cameras and recorders. This policy explains how Monvif accesses and handles information.

Information stored on your device

Monvif stores camera names, network addresses, ports, discovered stream profiles, multi-view layouts, and app preferences locally on your device. Camera usernames and passwords are stored using the secure credential storage provided by your operating system.

Camera connections

The app communicates directly with cameras and recorders that you configure. It uses local network discovery, ONVIF requests, and the stream addresses supplied by those devices. Mimigo does not proxy, receive, or retain those connections.

Connection security depends on the protocol and configuration supported by your camera or recorder. You are responsible for the security of devices and networks you connect to Monvif.

Video, audio, and snapshots

Live video and audio are processed on your device for playback. Monvif does not upload camera feeds to Mimigo. When you take a snapshot, the image is saved on your device. On Android, Monvif also places the image in the device gallery under Pictures/Monvif.

If you choose to open or share a snapshot with another application, that application handles the copy under its own privacy practices.

Data collection and sharing

Monvif does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Mimigo-operated account service. Mimigo does not sell personal information. The app does not send camera credentials, camera feeds, snapshots, or app activity to Mimigo.

Permissions and local network access

Monvif uses network, Wi-Fi state, and multicast access to connect to cameras and discover ONVIF devices on the local network. These permissions support camera monitoring and are not used for advertising or user tracking.

Retention and deletion

Camera configuration remains on your device until you remove the camera or clear the app's data. Stored credentials are removed when their recorder is removed. Snapshots remain until you delete them from Monvif or the device gallery. Uninstalling the app removes its private local data, while copies saved or shared outside the app may remain in those locations.

Children's privacy

Monvif is not directed to children under 13, and Mimigo does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving the site under its own privacy statement. Mimigo does not add analytics or advertising cookies to this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Monvif's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Mimigo through the public website issue tracker.
